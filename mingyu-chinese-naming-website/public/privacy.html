Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains what information Mingyu Naming Studio collects, how we use it, and how payment and technical providers process data when you use our AI-powered Chinese naming service, memberships, or credit packs.

Last updated: 2026-08-14

1. Information You Provide

When you use our service, you may provide your current name, birth date and time, birthplace time zone, gender preference, and naming wishes. If account registration, memberships, or credit packs are enabled, you may also provide an email address, billing country, and limited account information used to manage access, credits, invoices, renewals, and support.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To generate your personalized Chinese naming result.
  • To process and confirm payments through PayPal and other payment processors we may support.
  • To manage memberships, credit balances, invoices, renewals, cancellations, and fulfillment records.
  • To operate, secure, and improve the website.
  • To respond to customer support requests and resolve technical issues.

3. Payment Processing

Payments are processed by third-party providers such as PayPal, and we may add other processors for card payments in the future. We do not store your full card details or PayPal credentials on our servers. Payment providers may collect information necessary to complete payment, manage recurring billing, prevent fraud, and comply with legal requirements under their own privacy policies.

4. AI and Hosting Providers

To generate naming content, we may securely send the information required for generation to our model provider, such as DeepSeek or OpenAI, depending on the active configuration. Our site may also use service providers including Render for hosting and Cloudflare for DNS, security, and content delivery.

5. Retention

We do not intentionally build personal profiles from naming requests beyond what is needed to provide the service. Naming request data may be retained briefly in server memory or logs for security, debugging, payment reconciliation, abuse prevention, and proof of digital fulfillment. If memberships or credits are enabled, we may retain account-level billing and usage records needed to manage subscriptions, credit balances, invoices, and support.

If you do not want to submit a full personal name, you may enter only a surname or partial name.

6. Cookies and Technical Data

Our providers may use cookies, security logs, and technical identifiers such as IP address, browser information, and device metadata to secure the site, prevent fraud, and improve performance.

7. International Visitors

Because our infrastructure and providers may operate internationally, your information may be processed outside your country of residence. By using the service, you understand that such transfers may occur where needed to provide the service securely.

8. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, or restriction of personal information. To make a request, contact us using the support address listed below.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, payment issues, membership inquiries, cancellation requests, or data requests, contact us at support@chinesenamepro.com.

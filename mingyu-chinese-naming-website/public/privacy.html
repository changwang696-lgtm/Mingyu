Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains what information Mingyu Naming Studio collects, how we use it, and how payment and technical providers process data when you use our AI-powered Chinese naming service.

Last updated: 2026-08-17

1. Information You Provide

When you use our service, you may provide your current name, birth date and time, birthplace time zone, gender preference, naming wishes, and a delivery email. We use this information to generate the result, save the order for recovery, and help with support if needed.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To generate your personalized Chinese naming result.
  • To process and confirm payments through PayPal and other payment processors we may support.
  • To manage guest orders, digital fulfillment, PDF delivery, email delivery, and order recovery records.
  • To operate, secure, and improve the website.
  • To respond to customer support requests and resolve technical issues.

3. Payment Processing

When checkout is active, payments are processed by third-party providers such as PayPal, and we may add other processors for card payments in the future. We do not store your full card details or PayPal credentials on our servers. Payment providers may collect information necessary to complete payment, prevent fraud, and comply with legal requirements under their own privacy policies.

4. AI and Hosting Providers

To generate naming content, we may securely send the information required for generation to our model provider, such as DeepSeek or OpenAI, depending on the active configuration. Our site may also use service providers including Render for hosting and Cloudflare for DNS, security, and content delivery.

5. Retention

We do not intentionally build personal profiles from naming requests beyond what is needed to provide the service. Naming request data and guest order records may be retained for security, debugging, payment reconciliation, abuse prevention, proof of digital fulfillment, customer support, and order recovery.

If you do not want to submit a full personal name, you may enter only a surname or partial name.

6. Cookies and Technical Data

Our providers may use cookies, security logs, and technical identifiers such as IP address, browser information, and device metadata to secure the site, prevent fraud, and improve performance.

7. International Visitors

Because our infrastructure and providers may operate internationally, your information may be processed outside your country of residence. By using the service, you understand that such transfers may occur where needed to provide the service securely.

8. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, or restriction of personal information. To make a request, contact us using the support address listed below.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, payment issues, cancellation requests, delivery questions, or data requests, contact us at support@chinesenamepro.com.

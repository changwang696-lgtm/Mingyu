Cancellation Policy

This Cancellation Policy explains how recurring memberships, renewals, and credit-based access are cancelled at Mingyu Naming Studio.

Last updated: 2026-08-14

1. Monthly Memberships

If we offer a recurring membership, it renews automatically on the interval shown at checkout until cancelled. The checkout page and billing confirmation should show the renewal timing, recurring amount, and the plan name.

2. How to Cancel

  • Use the recurring billing controls provided by your payment method when available.
  • Use any member billing portal or account cancellation controls we provide when they are available.
  • Contact support@chinesenamepro.com and request cancellation using the email tied to your purchase.

3. When Cancellation Takes Effect

Cancellation stops future renewals. Unless required by law or expressly approved by us, cancellation does not automatically refund the current billing period or undo credits already delivered for that period.

4. One-Time Credit Packs

One-time credit packs do not auto-renew. They do not need cancellation because they are not subscriptions, stored cash balance, or wallet funds. Any validity period for one-time credits will be disclosed on the pricing or checkout page.

5. Billing Questions

If you think a renewal was charged in error, contact us promptly at support@chinesenamepro.com with your transaction reference and billing email so we can review it.

Cancellation Policy

This Cancellation Policy explains how payment cancellation works for Mingyu's current one-time digital naming service.

Last updated: 2026-08-17

1. Current Service Model

Mingyu currently sells one-time digital naming results through guest checkout. The current checkout does not rely on an automatically renewing subscription for delivery of the paid naming result.

2. How to Cancel

  • Before payment is completed, you may cancel directly on the PayPal page.
  • If you cancel on PayPal, PayPal returns you to our site and the guest order remains saved for later recovery or another attempt.
  • If you believe a payment completed incorrectly, contact support@chinesenamepro.com with the email tied to your purchase.

3. When Cancellation Takes Effect

Cancelling before payment confirmation stops that payment attempt. If payment has already been confirmed and the customized digital result has been delivered, cancellation does not automatically create a refund. Refunds are handled under our Refund Policy.

4. No Physical Shipment

Checkout covers a personalized digital result only. There is no physical shipment to stop or recall after cancellation.

5. Billing Questions

If you think a payment was charged in error, contact us promptly at support@chinesenamepro.com with your transaction reference, order ID, and billing email so we can review it.

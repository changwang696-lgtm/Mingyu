Delivery & Fulfillment Policy

This page explains how Mingyu fulfills paid guest orders for personalized digital naming results.

Last updated: 2026-08-17

1. What You Purchase

Mingyu sells one-time digital naming services. The current editions are a Simple Edition and a Complete Edition. We do not ship physical goods as part of checkout.

2. Order Creation Before Payment

Before you leave our website for PayPal, we create a pending guest order that stores the information required for fulfillment, recovery, and support. This allows us to reopen your result later if the page is closed or refreshed.

3. Payment Processing

Payment is processed by PayPal. If payment is cancelled, PayPal returns you to our site and the guest order remains saved for later recovery. If payment succeeds, PayPal returns you to our payment return page so we can continue fulfillment.

4. Fulfillment Timing

After successful payment confirmation, Mingyu generates and restores your personalized result on the website. In normal circumstances, delivery begins immediately after the PayPal return flow completes.

If fulfillment is delayed because of a temporary technical issue, contact us at support@chinesenamepro.com. We will review the order and either complete delivery or handle the issue under our Refund Policy.

5. How Delivery Happens

  • The result is shown on the Mingyu website after payment.
  • A PDF download is made available for fulfilled guest orders.
  • An email is sent to the delivery email provided at checkout with the order ID and recovery links.
  • An order lookup page is available if you need to reopen the result later.

6. No Physical Shipping

Checkout covers digital delivery only. No shipping address is required, and no physical item is dispatched as part of the paid naming service.

7. Support

For payment issues, delivery problems, or order recovery help, contact support@chinesenamepro.com and include your order ID and payment email.

Refund Policy

This Refund Policy applies to our customized digital naming services offered through Mingyu Naming Studio.

Last updated: 2026-08-17

1. Customized Digital Service

Our naming reports and AI naming outputs are personalized digital products generated specifically for each request. Because of this, orders are generally non-refundable once the customized result has been successfully generated and delivered on screen or inside the user account.

2. Refunds We May Approve

  • Duplicate charges for the same order.
  • Technical failure where payment was captured but the result could not be delivered.
  • Incorrect charge amount caused by a system error.
  • A confirmed payment problem that we cannot resolve through delivery or order recovery.

3. Cases Usually Not Eligible

  • You changed your mind after the customized result was generated.
  • You prefer different name styles, tones, or interpretations after delivery.
  • You entered incomplete or incorrect input details that changed the result.
  • You completed payment, received the customized result, and later changed your mind.

4. Delivery First

If payment succeeds but the website does not immediately open your result once checkout is active, contact us before purchasing again. We would rather complete the saved order, resend delivery links, or restore the PDF than have you pay twice.

Our order recovery flow and support email are part of how we fulfill customized digital orders.

5. How to Request Help

If you believe a refund is justified, contact us within 7 days of purchase at support@chinesenamepro.com and include your transaction reference, the email used for payment, and a short explanation of the issue.

6. Processing Time

If we approve a refund, it will be issued to the original PayPal payment method. Timing depends on PayPal and your payment provider.

If your payment succeeds but generation fails, contact us before purchasing again. We would rather fix the order than have you pay twice.

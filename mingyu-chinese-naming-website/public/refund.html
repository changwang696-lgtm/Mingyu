Refund Policy

This Refund Policy applies to our customized digital naming services, memberships, and credit packs purchased through Mingyu Naming Studio.

Last updated: 2026-08-14

1. Customized Digital Service

Our naming reports and AI naming outputs are personalized digital products generated specifically for each request. Because of this, orders are generally non-refundable once the customized result has been successfully generated and delivered on screen or inside the user account.

2. Refunds We May Approve

  • Duplicate charges for the same order.
  • Technical failure where payment was captured but the result could not be delivered.
  • Incorrect charge amount caused by a system error.
  • A membership renewal reported promptly where no credits or member benefits were used after renewal, if our review confirms the request.

3. Cases Usually Not Eligible

  • You changed your mind after the customized result was generated.
  • You prefer different name styles, tones, or interpretations after delivery.
  • You entered incomplete or incorrect input details that changed the result.
  • You purchased a one-time credit pack and have already used any of the included credits.

4. Memberships and Credit Packs

Monthly memberships renew automatically until cancelled. Cancelling stops future renewals but does not automatically refund the current billing period unless required by law or expressly approved by us. One-time credit packs do not auto-renew and are not treated as stored cash balance or wallet funds.

Where stated on the pricing page, unused recurring credits may reset at the end of the billing cycle. One-time credit packs may have their own validity period disclosed at purchase.

5. How to Request Help

If you believe a refund is justified, contact us within 7 days of purchase or renewal at support@chinesenamepro.com and include your transaction reference, the email used for payment, and a short explanation of the issue.

6. Processing Time

If we approve a refund, it will be issued to the original PayPal payment method. Timing depends on PayPal and your payment provider.

If your payment succeeds but generation fails, contact us before purchasing again. We would rather fix the order than have you pay twice.

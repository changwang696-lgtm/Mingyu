## 2. 可直接提交给 PayPal 的中文说明

我们的网站 `https://chinesenamepro.com` 提供一次性购买的个性化数字起名服务。  
用户在首页填写姓名、出生时间、时区和接收结果的邮箱后，可选择两档服务：

- Simple Edition：USD 2.99
- Complete Edition：USD 9.90

用户点击付款后，网站会先创建一条游客订单，再跳转到 PayPal 官方支付页面完成付款。  
付款成功后，PayPal 会把用户带回我们的网站，系统会自动恢复并交付数字结果。交付方式包括：

- 在网站内显示结果页
- 提供 PDF 下载
- 向用户填写的邮箱发送订单号、结果链接和找回链接
- 提供订单找回页面，便于用户重新打开已付款订单

本网站当前销售的是数字化、个性化内容服务，不涉及实体物流或实物发货。  
我们已在网站公开展示并可访问以下页面：

- Terms of Service
- Privacy Policy
- Refund Policy
- Cancellation Policy
- Delivery & Fulfillment Policy
- Order Lookup 页面

客服邮箱为 `support@chinesenamepro.com`，用于处理支付问题、交付问题和订单找回支持。

## 3. English version for PayPal review

Our website, `https://chinesenamepro.com`, sells one-time personalized digital naming services.

Before payment, the buyer fills in their name details, birth information, time zone, and a delivery email on the website. The buyer can then choose one of two editions:

- Simple Edition: USD 2.99
- Complete Edition: USD 9.90

When the buyer clicks to pay, our site first creates a pending guest order and then redirects the buyer to the official PayPal checkout page. After successful payment, PayPal returns the buyer to our website. We then fulfill the order by restoring and delivering the personalized digital result.

Fulfillment includes:

- displaying the result on the website,
- providing a PDF download,
- sending an email with the order ID, result link, and recovery link,
- and providing an order lookup page so the buyer can reopen the paid order later.

This is a digital personalized service only. We do not ship physical goods.

The website publicly displays the following pages:

- Terms of Service
- Privacy Policy
- Refund Policy
- Cancellation Policy
- Delivery & Fulfillment Policy
- Order Lookup page

Customer support is available at `support@chinesenamepro.com` for payment, delivery, and order recovery issues.
